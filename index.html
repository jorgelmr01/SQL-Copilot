Welcome to SQL Copilot

Your AI-powered assistant for generating, optimizing, and managing SQL queries with an intelligent schema at your fingertips.

The Main Value

Build a progressive Schema ERD that grows with your team's knowledge, while keeping all your SQL queries organized in one searchable, centralized place.

account_tree

Progressive Schema ERD

Build your database knowledge over time. Visually map tables, columns, and relationships in an interactive diagram that becomes your team's source of truth.

folder_data

Centralized SQL Library

Keep all your team's queries in one searchable, organized place. Navigate folders, categorize by table type (Raw, Silver, Gold, Diamond, Black), and reference queries in chats.

AI-Powered Generation

Describe what you need in plain English. The AI creates a logic preview, suggests variable mappings from your schema, and generates optimized SQL.

Smart Variable Mapping

AI suggests real schema variables for your queries. Select from your ERD, accept suggestions, or define custom names - all in an intuitive card-based interface.
